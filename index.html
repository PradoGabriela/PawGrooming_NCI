How it all began

At the annual Dublin Pet Expo, the air buzzed with excited barks and soft purrs. Liam stood near the agility ring with Barnaby, his energetic Irish Setter, while Maeve sat nearby with Clover, her emerald-eyed British Shorthair. When Barnaby’s wagging tail knocked over Maeve’s grooming kit, Liam rushed to help. As they gathered the brushes, Clover curiously booped Barnaby’s wet nose, sealing an instant bond. Just then, they spotted a shivering, mud-caked stray terrier cowering behind the vendor tents. Without hesitation, Liam wrapped the trembling pup in his coat while Maeve gently washed away the grime with her show supplies. Inspired by that rescue, their friendship blossomed over rainy walks along the Wicklow coast. Combining Liam’s patience with dogs and Maeve’s gentle touch with cats, they opened Paw Animal Care in a cozy cottage—with their newly adopted terrier happily greeting every guest at the door.
