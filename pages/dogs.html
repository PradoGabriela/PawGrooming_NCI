Dog Grooming Services

Tailored grooming services for every dog

Small Breed Grooming

Ideal for breeds including:

Chihuahuas Yorkshire Terriers Shih Tzus Pomeranians Dachshunds

Small dogs receive a gentle grooming session suited to their size and coat type. This includes:

  • Warm bath
  • Blow-dry and brush-out
  • Haircut or trim where needed
  • Gentle care around eyes, ears, paws and face

Extra pampering: Long-haired breeds receive extra brushing to remove knots and prevent matting. Nail trimming and paw cleaning available to keep your dog comfortable.

Medium Breed Grooming

Ideal for breeds including:

Cocker Spaniels Beagles Border Collies Corgis Miniature Schnauzers

Our medium breed grooming service is suited to dogs with different coat lengths and textures. Each session includes:

  • Professional bath and blow-dry
  • Thorough brush-out
  • Breed-suited trim
  • Extra attention to problem areas

Special care: Dogs with thicker or longer coats receive extra brushing to remove loose hair and tangles, especially behind ears, around legs and under the belly.

Book a session

Large Breed Grooming

Ideal for breeds including:

Golden Retrievers German Shepherds Labradors Siberian Huskies Bernese Mountain Dogs

Larger dogs receive a full grooming session, including:

  • Bath, blow-dry and brush-out
  • Coat trim where needed
  • Paws, ears, belly and tail checked and tidied

Special coat care: Thick and double coats, such as those of Huskies, German Shepherds and Golden Retrievers, receive extra attention to help remove loose undercoat and reduce shedding. Extra time is allowed for washing and drying, leaving coats clean and easier to manage.
