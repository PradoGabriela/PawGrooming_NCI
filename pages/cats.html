Cat Grooming Services

Tailored grooming services for every cat

No Hair

Suitable for hairless cats that need regular skin care to keep their skin clean and healthy.

Our grooming session focuses on caring for your cat's skin, including a gentle bath to remove excess oils and dirt. The skin is carefully cleaned and dried, with extra attention given to areas such as the face, ears, paws and skin folds. Nail trimming and ear cleaning are also included to help keep your cat comfortable and clean.

From €45

Book a session

Short Hair

Suitable for cats with short, smooth coats that need regular cleaning and basic coat care.

The grooming session includes a gentle bath, towel drying and brushing to remove loose hair and dirt from the coat. Extra attention is given to cleaning the ears, trimming the nails and checking the paws. Brushing also helps reduce loose hair around the home and keeps the coat looking clean and healthy.

From €55

Book a session

Long Hair

Suitable for cats with longer, thicker coats that need extra brushing and coat maintenance.

The grooming session includes a bath, careful drying and a thorough brush-out to remove loose hair and prevent tangles. Extra time is spent checking areas that are more likely to develop knots, such as behind the ears, under the legs, around the belly and near the tail. A small trim can also be provided where needed to keep the coat neat and easier to manage.

From €65
